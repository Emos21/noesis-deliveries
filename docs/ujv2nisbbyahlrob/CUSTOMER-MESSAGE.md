# The WhatsApp message for your customers

Copy the message you want, paste it into WhatsApp, send. Nothing here needs
editing except the one spot marked `[PASTE YOUR LINK HERE]`.

Two small things first, so nothing goes wrong:

- You do not have a link yet. The page is still a file on your phone or
  computer, not on the internet. Use message 2 until it is online, then switch
  to message 1. Say the word and we will put it online and send you the link.
- The prices on the page are still the sample ones. Put your real prices in
  before you send any of this to customers.
- The message names manicure, pedicure, gel, acrylic and nail art. Read that
  line once and cross off anything you do not actually do.

---

Hi, it's Wanjiru.

My prices are now all on one little page you can open any time:

each one, so you always know before you sit down.

Walk in whenever you like, or send me a message and I will keep a slot for

Thank you for trusting me with your nails.

A note on sending it: WhatsApp does not like the same message going out to
hundreds of people at once from a normal account. Use a Broadcast list and
keep it under about 100 people at a time, or paste it to each customer you
talk to often. It reads better coming to them one to one anyway.

If you like a bit of colour in your messages, add an emoji or two yourself
where it feels like you. The words are written to work with or without them.
