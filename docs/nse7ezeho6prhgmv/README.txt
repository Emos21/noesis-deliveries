Wairimu Braids price list
=========================

Wairimu-Braids-Price-List.pdf is the file to print.

It is one page, A4 size, black text on white with a warm brown accent, so it
prints cleanly on an ordinary printer and does not use much ink. When you
print it, choose "Actual size" or 100 percent rather than "Fit to page", so
the spacing stays as designed.

What is on it: all twelve services with their prices, grouped as braids and
twists, locs and weaves, and care and extras. The consultation shows as FREE.
The opening hours panel at the bottom reads Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm,
closed on Sunday. Prices are marked as Kenya shillings once under the title,
so the list itself stays easy to read.

Not included: there is no phone number, address or social media handle on the
list, because none were sent. If you would like them added at the bottom, send
them across and they can go on.

To change a price later, send the change and an updated PDF can be made.
