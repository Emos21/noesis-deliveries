Every piece of work on file in this environment, one row each, with whose it
is. Compiled 2026-09-05 by reading the files in this environment. Nothing was
changed except this page being added.

## How to handle this page

Read together, the rows below are the studio's client list with trades and
locations attached. That makes this an internal studio record, for the founder
and studio staff.

The studio has already put its disclosure line in writing twice. To Kariuki:
"You have your own isolated client environment named qa31-client. Nobody else's
data is in it and yours is not in anyone else's"
(`qa31-client/WHAT-WE-HOLD-ABOUT-KARIUKI.md`). And for Kiptoo, an email was held
back because sending it "would disclose Kiptoo's interest to another client,
against the confidentiality line already set" (`qa40-mate/logfile.md`). A person
gets their own rows, not everyone's. This page as a whole is not a client
handout.

## How ownership was worked out

Each name below comes only from what that folder says about itself: a client
line in a logfile, a name in a README, a business name printed on the page, or a
name written in a note. Where a folder never names anybody, the register says
that instead of guessing.

The organization's own job ledger, which records who asked for each job, does
not live in this environment and could not be read from here. So ownership here
is what the work itself records, which is not the same thing as a verified
customer record. Two cautions that follow from that:

- In the four property notes (`qa10-client`, `qa10-peterlike`, `qa11-client`,
  `qa11-peterlike`) the person named is a caretaker, a landlord or a gate
  contact written about in the note. The note does not say who asked for it.
- The folder names are QA round names, `qaNN` plus a role word, and several
  files describe themselves that way, for example "this QA client environment"
  in `qa18-client/logfile.md` and "Client space: Amerissa QA6". Treat the role
  word in a folder name as a label on the round, not as proof of who a person is.

## What this register cannot tell you

- Who asked for each job. Ownership above is who the work is for, as the work
  itself records it. The asker is recorded in the organization's job ledger,
  outside this environment.
- Whether a job was paid for, delivered or accepted. Only `qa38-client`,
  `qa40-probe` and `qa41-link` carry a `.delivery` copy, which shows a published
  handover. Everything else has no delivery record here either way.
- Whether the people named are current clients. Several folders are open on
  their own terms: `qa40-mate` is still waiting on Kiptoo, `qa42-client` and
  `qa43-client` still hold example prices, and `qa28-client` records a request to
  email a file that was refused because sending is not permitted and no client
  mail identity is configured.
