# Mumbi Car Wash, price page

This folder holds your price page. There is one page: `index.html`.

## How to look at it

Double click `index.html`. It opens in any browser, on a phone or a laptop.
It does not need internet, an app, or a login. It works offline.

## What we guessed, and what you should correct

You did not send us your figures, so we filled the page with sensible Kikuyu
prices as a starting point. Please correct these five things:

1. **Your business name.** The page says "Mumbi Car Wash" in three places.
2. **Every price.** All of them are guesses.
3. **Your phone number.** It says 0700 000 000 at the bottom, in two places.
4. **Your location line and opening hours** at the bottom.
5. **The four lines in the "Good to know" box**, especially the M-Pesa line.

## How to change a price

1. Right click `index.html` and open it with a text editor (Text Editor,
   Notepad, or anything similar). Do not open it with Word.
2. Find the number you want to change. Each service is on its own line.
3. Type the new number over the old one. Leave everything else alone,
   especially the pointy brackets.
4. Save the file, then refresh the page in the browser.

There is a note at the top of `index.html` that says the same thing, so you
have it in front of you while you edit.

## Vehicle sizes

Prices run in three columns: saloon, SUV and wagon, van and pickup. If you
charge one price no matter the vehicle, tell us and we will collapse it to a
single column.

## Printing it

The page is set up to print cleanly on one sheet, so you can pin it up at the
wash. Use File, then Print, in the browser.

## Putting it online

It is not online yet. When your prices are final, tell us and we will publish
it at a link you can send on WhatsApp.
