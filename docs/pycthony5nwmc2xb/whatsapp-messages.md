# Words to send parents on WhatsApp

Copy any message below, change the parts in [square brackets], and send.
Each one is short on purpose, because long messages do not get read.

Fill in these three things once and the rest is easy:

- [Shop name]
- [Shop number and street], near [nearest landmark], Kariobangi
- [07XX XXX XXX]

---

## 1. First message to a parent, English

Hello. I am [your name] from [Shop name] in Kariobangi. I sew school
uniforms to measure: shirts, shorts, trousers, skirts, tunics and games
kit, plus repairs and name labels. Tell me the school and your child's
age and I will send you the price and the day it will be ready.

## 2. First message to a parent, Kiswahili

Habari. Mimi ni [jina lako] wa [Shop name] hapa Kariobangi. Nashona
sare za shule kwa kipimo cha mtoto: shati, kaptura, suruali, sketi,
tunic na nguo za michezo. Pia narekebisha na nashona jina la mtoto
ndani. Niambie shule na umri wa mtoto nikutumie bei na siku ya kuchukua.

## 3. Start of term reminder, send about two weeks before school opens

Schools open on [date]. If your child needs a new uniform, bring the old
one or the school colour this week and it will be ready before opening
day. Later than that and the shop is full. [Shop name], Kariobangi,
[07XX XXX XXX].

Kiswahili version:

Shule zinafunguliwa [tarehe]. Kama mtoto anahitaji sare mpya, lete sare
ya zamani au rangi ya shule wiki hii ili iwe tayari kabla ya kufungua.
Ukichelewa, kazi inakuwa nyingi. [Shop name], Kariobangi,
[07XX XXX XXX].

## 4. When someone asks how much

A [item] is from KSh [amount], and a full set is from KSh [amount]. The
final price depends on the school pattern and the size. Half deposit to
start, the rest when you collect. M-Pesa or cash.

## 5. Asking for measurements

To sew for your child I need four things: age, height, chest and waist.
If you cannot measure, bring the child to the shop, measuring is free.
Or send a photo of an old uniform laid flat and I will work from that.

## 6. Confirming an order

Noted. [Quantity] [item] for [child's name], [school]. Total KSh
[amount], deposit KSh [amount] to [M-Pesa number or till]. It will be
ready on [day]. I will message you when it is done.

## 7. When the work is ready

Good news. [Child's name]'s uniform is ready. You can collect it at
[Shop number and street], near [nearest landmark], any day from
[8am to 6pm]. Balance is KSh [amount]. If anything does not fit I will
adjust it free.

## 8. Repairs and growing children

Do not buy a new uniform yet. If the sleeves or hem are short, bring it
and I will let it out. Torn seams, zips and buttons are also small
money. Cheaper than a new set.

Kiswahili version:

Usinunue sare mpya bado. Kama mikono au pindo imekuwa fupi, lete
niongeze. Pia narekebisha mishono iliyoachana, zipu na vifungo kwa bei
ndogo. Ni rahisi kuliko kununua sare mpya.

## 9. For a school, a head teacher or a children's home

Good afternoon. I am [your name] of [Shop name] in Kariobangi and I sew
school uniforms. I can supply your pupils' uniforms to measure, from one
set to a whole class, at KSh [amount] a set with delivery to the school.
May I bring a sample for you to see this week?

## 10. Asking a happy parent to spread the word

Thank you for the order. If another parent asks where you got the
uniform, please share my number: [07XX XXX XXX]. It helps my shop a lot.

## 11. WhatsApp Status posts, one line each

- Uniforms sewn to measure. Kariobangi. [07XX XXX XXX].
- School opens [date]. Bring the uniform now, not on the last day.
- Sleeves too short? I let them out for small money. Do not buy new.
- One set or a whole class. [Shop name], near [nearest landmark].

## 12. Your WhatsApp About line

School uniforms sewn to measure, Kariobangi. Repairs and alterations.
Open Monday to Saturday.

## Two small habits that bring more work

1. Save every parent's number with the school and child's name, for
   example "Wanjiru, Class 4, St Mary". Then next January you can send
   message number 3 to all of them.
2. Take a photo of every finished uniform before it leaves the shop and
   put it on your Status. Parents buy what they can see.
