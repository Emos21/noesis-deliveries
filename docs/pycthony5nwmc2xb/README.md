# Your flyer page and your WhatsApp words

Two things are here for you.

1. `index.html` is your flyer page. Double click it and it opens in any
   phone or computer browser. It works without internet.
2. `whatsapp-messages.md` is the wording to send parents. Open it, copy a
   message, change the parts in [square brackets], send.

## Filling in your details on the flyer

Open `index.html` in a browser first. Every place that needs your
details is marked with a **yellow highlight**. This is the list:

- your shop name (top of the page, and again at the very bottom)
- your shop number and street
- your nearest landmark
- your phone number
- your prices, five lines
- how many days a single set takes
- how many days a bulk order takes
- your opening hours and your closed day

To change them: right click `index.html`, open it with a text editor
(Text Editor, Notepad, or ask us), find the words you want to replace,
type yours over them. Save. Refresh the browser.

## Two spots that need your number in full

The **Message on WhatsApp** button and the **Call the shop** button at the
bottom of the page need your number written the international way, with no zero and no
spaces. If your number is 0712 345 678, you write `254712345678`.

In the text editor, search for `2547XXXXXXXX`. It appears twice. Replace
both with your number, for example `254712345678`.

Until you do that, the two buttons will not dial anyone.

## Printing it as a paper flyer

Open the page and press Ctrl and P together. It is already set up to
print tidily on one A4 page in black and white, so it is cheap to
photocopy for the shop window, the school gate and the shopping centre.

## Sharing it as a link

Right now the page lives only on this computer. If you want a link you
can paste into WhatsApp so parents can open it on their phones, tell
Amerissa Studio and we will put it online for you.

## A note on the prices

We left the prices blank on purpose. We do not know your costs, and a
wrong price on a flyer is worse than no price. Put your own "from"
prices in, and keep the line that says the final price depends on the
school pattern, so you are never trapped by a printed number.
