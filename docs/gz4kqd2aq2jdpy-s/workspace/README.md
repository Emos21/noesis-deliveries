# Your shoe price page

Grace, here is the page you asked for. One page, three lists, made to be
held up on your phone at the stall.

Two files:

- `index.html` is the page. This is the one you show customers.
- `prices-for-whatsapp.txt` is the same list as plain writing, for when you
  want to paste the prices into a WhatsApp chat instead of showing a screen.

## The prices in it are examples

We do not know what you charge, so the page starts with the kind of prices a
Gikomba stall tends to ask. They are there so you can see the shape of the
list, not because they are yours.

There is a yellow bar at the top of the page saying exactly that. It
disappears by itself the first time you save your own prices, so a customer
never sees it once you have set the page up.

## Put your own prices in, on the phone

You do not need a computer and you do not need to touch any code.

1. Open the page on your phone.
2. Tap the brown button at the top, "Set my prices".
3. Every shoe now has a box for its name, a small note, and its price. Type
   over any of them.
4. To take out a shoe you do not sell, tap "Remove this shoe" under it.
   To add one, tap "Add a shoe here" at the bottom of that list.
5. You can also change the three headings, the name at the top, your phone
   number and the last line.
6. Tap "Save".

Your prices are then kept on that phone, and the page opens with them every
time. The name at the top cannot be left empty, so if you clear it the old
name comes back.

If you want the example list again, there is a small "Start again with the
example list" link next to Save.

## Keep the page on your phone

Open `index.html` once in your phone's browser, then use the browser menu
and choose "Add to Home screen". After that it is one tap from your home
screen, and it works with no internet and no airtime.

The prices live in that one browser on that one phone. If you clear your
browser data, or you get a new phone, they are gone. So while you are in the
editing screen, scroll to the bottom and tap "Save a copy to my files" once
you are happy. That puts a small file in your downloads that "Open a saved
copy" can read back later. If your phone refuses to save that file, do not
worry, your prices on the phone are not affected.

## Showing customers

- Hold up the page. It is made for a small screen: large numbers, one list
  under the other, no pictures to load.
- Or send the prices as writing. Open `prices-for-whatsapp.txt`, copy
  everything under the dotted line, and paste it into the chat.
- If you want it on paper for the stall, open the page on a computer and
  print it. The buttons and the yellow bar do not print.

## If you want changes

Send the studio your real prices, or any kind of shoe we have missed or
named wrongly, and we will update the page and the WhatsApp version for you.
